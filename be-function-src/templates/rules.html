Welcome to {{ site_name }}! These guidelines describe how we expect users to behave when interacting on our site. By participating (commenting, posting, sharing), you agree to follow these rules.

1. Be Respectful

You should treat other members of our community with respect. Do not harass, bully, intimidate or discriminate against others. Constructive disagreement is fine — personal attacks or hateful language are not.

2. Use Appropriate Language and Content

Avoid profanity, obscenity, or content that is sexually explicit, violent, or otherwise inappropriate for a general audience. If you share images or media, they must be appropriate for public viewing.

3. No Hate Speech, Harassment or Discrimination

We do not tolerate content or behavior that targets a person or group based on race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, age or other protected characteristic.

4. No Illegal or Dangerous Behavior

Do not post or promote content that is unlawful, or encourages illegal or harmful behavior (including self-harm, violence, or exploitation).

5. No Spam, Fake Engagement or Misleading Behavior

Do not post unsolicited promotions, repetitive content, or try to manipulate user engagement (e.g., fake comments, bots, excessive linking). Be honest and transparent.

6. Respect Intellectual Property

Only share content you have rights to. Do not repost someone else’s work without permission. If you use copyrighted material, provide proper attribution or ensure you have the right to use it.

7. Privacy & Personal Information

Do not share private or sensitive personal information about others (e.g., precise location, financial data, medical details) without consent. Respect people’s privacy.

8. Enforcement

Violations of these guidelines may result in actions such as comment removal, account suspension, or content deletion, at our discretion. Repeated or severe violations may result in permanent bans.

9. Changes to These Guidelines

We may update these guidelines at any time. The latest version will always be available at this page, and your continued participation constitutes acceptance of the revised rules.

Contact

If you have questions or would like to report a violation, please contact us at the Contacts page of {{ site_name }}.
