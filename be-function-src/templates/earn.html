Monetization

Authors may include a donation option in their profile. When enabled, a donation button can appear on their content, allowing readers to contribute directly.

Payments are handled by third-party providers and go directly to the author. {{ site_name }} does not take a commission unless explicitly stated otherwise.

More donation providers may be supported in the future.
